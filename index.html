About the test: format, pass marks, the day

The test at a glance

66 multiple-choice questions in three parts, in 60 minutes. Each part is marked on its own, so you must reach the pass mark in every part. A perfect score on signs will not save a fail on controls.

You can get only {{p.miss}} wrong.
How you are tested
Multiple choice, on a computer or on paper at a driving licence testing centre (DLTC). On paper you get a question book, an answer sheet, a road sign chart and pictures of road layouts and vehicle controls.
Minimum age
16: motorcycle 125cc or less, no sidecar (Code 1).
17: light motor vehicle up to 3 500 kg (Code 2).
18: heavy vehicles (Code 3).
On the day
Apply on form LL1. Bring your ID or passport, two passport photos, and pass the eye test (or bring a valid optometrist test). The learner's licence lasts 24 months.
